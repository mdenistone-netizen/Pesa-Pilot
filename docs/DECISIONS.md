| DEC-019 | 2026-11-25 | **PIN protection.** Store only PBKDF2-SHA-256 (Web Crypto) of the PIN, with a unique 16-byte cryptographically random salt per PIN and the iteration count recorded with the hash. Importing keeps the importing device's own PIN. Repeated wrong entries are slowed down. The PIN's length (4–8) is stored so the keypad works as before.
