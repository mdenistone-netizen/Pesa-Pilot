# 00 · Research Data Notice (what the research team collects, and how it's protected)

This notice applies to everyone who takes part. Its key points are read aloud in the consent script (`02_CONSENT.md`), in English and Kiswahili. **Owner approval is needed before any of it is used.**

## 1. Two different kinds of information

| | **Participants' financial records in PesaPilot** | **Research information collected by the team** |
|---|---|---|
| Examples | Entries, balances, goals typed into the app | Screener answers, interview notes, test timings, scoring sheets, WhatsApp check-in replies, optional audio |
| Where it is | **Only on the participant's own phone** (or the team's test phone, with made-up numbers) | With the research team (§4) |
| Who can see it | Only the participant. **The team never receives it.** | Only the people listed in §3 |
| Deletion | The participant deletes it at any time (Settings → Delete all data) | On the schedule in §5, or earlier on request |

**WhatsApp replies are research information collected by the team.** They are *not* stored by PesaPilot, but they do pass through WhatsApp, and the team keeps them as described below.

## 2. What we collect and what we never ask for
**We collect (only what's needed):**
- **Screener:** income type, family support, borrowing (yes/no), chama/SACCO (yes/no), how they track money, language, area type. **Initials and a phone number only for scheduling.**
- **Interview notes and scoring sheets,** under a participant code (P01…), not a name.
- **Test results:** task times, errors and hesitations on the **test phone** with **made-up numbers**.
- **7-day trial (voluntary):** answers to three daily questions (approximate counts and a reason letter). Optionally, on day 7, the **number** of entries the participant reads out from their own screen.
- **Audio:** only if the participant agrees separately.

**We never ask for, and never accept if offered:**
- PINs or passwords
- ID numbers or ID documents
- M-Pesa messages, statements, or screenshots of them
- Bank or mobile-money account screenshots or statements
- Exact balances or exact amounts of personal transactions
- **Any screenshots at all.** On day 7 the participant may *read out* the number of entries, nothing more.

If someone sends a screenshot or message anyway, the team deletes it immediately without saving it, and reminds them it isn't needed.

## 3. Who can access research information
- **The owner** and, if hired, **one named interviewer**, bound by a written confidentiality note.
- **Claude** (the development assistant) sees **only anonymised summaries** that the owner chooses to share: participant codes, no names or phone numbers.
- **No one else.** No sharing with lenders, banks, advertisers or other organisations.

## 4. How it is stored
- **Notes, scoring sheets and the participant-code key** live in a **private folder** (for example a private Google Drive folder shared only with the people in §3), **never in the public GitHub repository**.
- **The code key** (code ↔ initials/phone) is a separate file, accessible only to the owner.
- **WhatsApp:** a **dedicated study phone number**. After each day's replies are copied to the scoring sheet (as codes), the chat messages are deleted from the study phone.
- **Audio:** stored on the recording device or the private folder only, until notes are written.

## 5. Deletion schedule

| Information | Deleted |
|---|---|
| Audio recordings | Within **7 days** of the interview, once notes are written |
| WhatsApp chats on the study phone | **Daily**, after copying the replies as codes; the last chat at the end of the trial |
| Phone numbers and the code key | Within **30 days** after the study ends (after the last thank-you payment) |
| Interview notes and scoring sheets (coded) | **6 months** after the study ends |
| Anonymised findings (no codes, no personal details) | Kept, as `VALIDATION_FINDINGS.md` |

## 6. Participants' choices
- Taking part, and **joining the 7-day trial**, is **voluntary**.
- Anyone can **skip questions, stop the interview, or leave the trial at any time, without giving a reason and without penalty.**
- **The thank-you is the same for everyone**, whatever they say and whether or not they complete every activity (`08_BUDGET_FOR_REVIEW.md`).
- Anyone can ask for their research information to be **deleted**, up until the anonymised findings are written. After that, they can no longer be identified in them.

## 7. Legal note
- This research handles personal information under **Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019** (consent, purpose limitation, minimisation, security, retention). This notice is written with those principles in mind, **but it is not legal advice**.
- If the study grows beyond this small pilot, or uses a hired research firm, the owner should get a short legal review.
