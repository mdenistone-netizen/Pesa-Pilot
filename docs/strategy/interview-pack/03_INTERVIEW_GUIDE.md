# 03 · Interview Guide (about 40 minutes)

## Golden rules for the interviewer
1. **Ask about the past, not the future.** "When did you last…?", "Walk me through…", "What happened next?"
2. **Never ask** "Would you use…?", "Would you pay…?" or "Do you like this idea?" These produce polite fiction.
3. **Specifics over generalities.** When someone says "usually", ask: "Tell me about the last time."
4. **Don't pitch PesaPilot** until the test script. Don't explain how the app works during the interview.
5. **Silence is fine.** Count to five before helping.
6. **Mark each note** as **[DID]** (actual past behaviour), **[SAYS]** (opinion or wish), or **[OBS]** (something you observed).

---

## A · Money coming in (5 min)
1. "Tell me how money came to you **last week**. What came in, from where, which day?" [DID]
   - *Probe:* Is last week normal? What does a bad week look like? A good one?
2. "Where does your money sit? M-Pesa, cash, bank, M-Shwari or KCB M-Pesa, SACCO, chama, somewhere at home?" [DID]
   - *Probe:* Which one do you use most for daily spending?

## B · The last time money ran out (8 min) → the core problem
3. "**When was the last time** money ran out before the next money came?" [DID]
   - *If they can't recall one:* "What about in the last 3 months?" (Record "none recalled" honestly.)
4. "Walk me through that day. What did you need to pay? What did you do?" [DID]
   - *Probe:* Fuliza? Borrowed from whom? Skipped something? Sold something?
5. "What did that cost you, in money and otherwise?" [DID]
6. "Looking back, when did you first realise money would be short?" [DID]
   - *Listen for:* surprise vs knowing early.

## C · How they check balances today (6 min) → research question 4
7. "**When did you last check** how much money you have? How did you check?" [DID]
   - *Probe:* \*334#, the M-Pesa app, the last M-Pesa message, a bank app, counting cash, a notebook.
8. "How often do you check? What makes you check?" [DID]
   - *Listen for:* before a purchase, after a payment, when worried, never.
9. "Was the amount ever **different from what you expected**? Tell me about the last time." [DID]
   - *Probe:* What did you do? Did you work out where the money went?

## D · Keeping track and forgotten transactions (10 min) → questions 1–3
10. "Have you **ever** tried to write down or record what you spend: notebook, phone notes, Excel, an app?" [DID]
    - **If yes:** "Can you describe how you do it?" *(Never ask to see bank, M-Pesa or other financial records; a paper notebook may be described, not photographed.)* [DID] "When did you last add something?" [DID] "How long did you keep it going?" [DID] "**What made you stop**, or what makes you skip days?" [DID]
    - **If no:** "What do you do instead to know where money went?" [DID] "What has stopped you from writing things down?" [SAYS, mark as opinion]
11. "Think of **yesterday**. What did you spend money on? Can you list them?" [DID]
    - *Observe:* how many they recall, and whether small items (fare, airtime, snacks, M-Pesa charges) are forgotten. [OBS]
12. "When you find money missing, how do you work out what you forgot?" [DID]
    - *Probe:* scrolling M-Pesa messages, the statement, asking family, giving up.
13. "Which kinds of spending are you most likely to forget?" [SAYS]
14. "Where are you usually when you pay for things? Could you type something then, or later?" [DID first: "Yesterday, when you paid for X, where were you?"]

## E · Commitments, family, chama, loans (6 min)
15. "What payments come every week or month that you **must** make?" (rent, school fees, chama, loans, KPLC…) [DID]
16. "How do you make sure that money is there when it's due?" [DID]
    - *Probe:* keep it in M-Pesa, a separate account, give it to someone, chama.
17. "**The last time** a relative asked for money: what happened, and how did you decide?" [DID]
18. "Do you lend money to people? Last time: was it repaid? How do you remember who owes you?" [DID]

## F · Tools and paying for things (5 min)
19. "Which apps or services did you pay for **last month**? How much?" (bundles, TV, music, betting, SACCO or app fees) [DID]
20. "Have you ever paid for anything to help manage money? What happened?" [DID]
21. *(Opinion only, low weight; VALIDATION_PLAN §6.1)* "For an app that showed exactly how much of your money is free to spend, at what monthly price would it seem too cheap to trust, a bargain, getting expensive, too expensive?" [SAYS]
