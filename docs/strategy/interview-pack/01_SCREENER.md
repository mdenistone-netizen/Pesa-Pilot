# 01 · Participant Screener (about 3 minutes, read aloud)

**Purpose:** reach a balanced group of **24 participants**. Most should be ordinary users, not finance enthusiasts.
**Record:** answers in the quota tracker below. Use **initials + phone last-3-digits only** while scheduling, then a participant code (P01…). Destroy contact details after the study.

## Questions
1. **How does money mostly come to you?** *(choose the main one)*
   - Salary / regular wage → **S**
   - Casual or daily work (boda, mjengo, market, house help, porter…) → **C**
   - Own business (kiosk, salon, agent, tailor, online selling…) → **B**
   - Freelance or gig work (design, ride-hailing, tutoring…) → **F**
   - Farming → **C** (if daily or seasonal sales) or **B**
   - Mostly support from others → note it; include at most 2
2. **Do you have children in school or other dependants at home?** Yes → **Young family** if under ~40 *(also counts in the main group)*
3. **Do you regularly send money to relatives outside your home?** Yes → **Family support**
4. **In the last 3 months, have you used Fuliza or any loan app, or borrowed from a shop or friend to get by?** Yes → **Borrower**
5. **Are you in a chama or a SACCO?** Yes → **Chama/SACCO**
6. **How do you keep track of your money today?**
   - Nothing / in my head → **Non-tracker**
   - Notebook or phone notes
   - The M-Pesa app statement
   - A budgeting app (which?) → **App user**
7. **Do you use an Android smartphone every day, and are you comfortable typing on it?** No → thank them and stop (the preview needs a smartphone)
8. **Which language would you like to talk in?** English / Kiswahili / Sheng mix
9. **Where do you live?** Nairobi / a town near Nairobi / another town or rural area

## Exclude (politely)
- People working on fintech or budgeting apps, or close friends and family of the team (at most 5 from the owner's wider circle in total)
- Under 18
