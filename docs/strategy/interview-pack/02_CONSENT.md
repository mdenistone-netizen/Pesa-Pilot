# 02 · Consent Script

Read aloud before anything else. Record **"consent: yes/no"** and **"audio: yes/no"** on the scoring sheet. Without a clear yes, thank the person, pay the thank-you, and stop.

## English
> "Thank you for giving us your time.
> We're building a phone app that helps people plan their money. Today we want to learn how **you** manage money now. There are no right or wrong answers, and **we are not selling anything.**
>
> We will **not** ask for your PIN, ID number, M-Pesa messages or statements, or your exact balances. You can skip any question, and you can stop at any time without giving a reason.
>
> Later, we'll ask you to try the app on **our** test phone using made-up numbers. We are testing the app, **not you**. If something is confusing, that's the app's fault and exactly what we need to know.
>
> We'll take written notes using a code, not your name. **May we take notes?**
> *(Optional:)* May we also record the audio? We'll delete it after writing our notes.
>
> You'll receive KSh ____ for your time, **whatever you say**, even if you stop early.
>
> Do you have any questions? Are you happy to continue?"

## Kiswahili (draft: **needs review by a fluent speaker before use**)
> "Asante kwa kutupa muda wako.
> Tunatengeneza programu ya simu inayosaidia watu kupanga pesa zao. Leo tunataka kujifunza jinsi **wewe** unavyosimamia pesa sasa hivi. Hakuna jibu sahihi au lisilo sahihi, na **hatukuuzii chochote.**
>
> **Hatutakuuliza** PIN yako, nambari ya kitambulisho, jumbe za M-Pesa au taarifa za akaunti, wala salio lako halisi. Unaweza kuruka swali lolote, na unaweza kusimama wakati wowote bila kueleza sababu.
>
> Baadaye tutakuomba ujaribu programu kwenye simu **yetu** ya majaribio ukitumia nambari za kubuni. Tunajaribu programu, **si wewe**. Kama kitu hakieleweki, ni kosa la programu, na hicho ndicho tunachohitaji kujua.
>
> Tutaandika maelezo kwa kutumia namba ya siri, si jina lako. **Tunaweza kuandika maelezo?**
> *(Hiari:)* Tunaweza pia kurekodi sauti? Tutaifuta baada ya kuandika maelezo.
>
> Utapokea KSh ____ kwa muda wako, **bila kujali utakachosema**, hata ukisimama mapema.
>
> Una swali lolote? Uko tayari kuendelea?"
