# 02 · Consent Scripts (English and Kiswahili, same protections)

Read aloud before anything else. Both languages contain **the same 9 points**, numbered so they can be checked side by side.

**Record on the scoring sheet:**
- consent: yes/no;
- audio: yes/no;
- trial: yes/no/not offered.

Without a clear yes to the interview, pay the thank-you and stop.

> The Kiswahili text is a careful draft and **must be checked by a fluent speaker** for natural wording, without changing the meaning of any point, before use.

## Interview consent

| # | English | Kiswahili |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Purpose | "Thank you for your time. We are building a phone app to help people plan their money. Today we want to learn how **you** manage money now. There are no right or wrong answers, and **we are not selling anything.**" | "Asante kwa muda wako. Tunatengeneza programu ya simu ya kusaidia watu kupanga pesa zao. Leo tunataka kujifunza jinsi **wewe** unavyosimamia pesa sasa hivi. Hakuna jibu sahihi wala lisilo sahihi, na **hatukuuzii chochote.**" |
| 2 What we never ask | "We will **never** ask for your PIN or passwords, ID number or documents, M-Pesa messages or statements, bank details, **any screenshots**, or your exact balances. Please don't share them, even if you'd like to help." | "**Hatutawahi** kukuuliza PIN au nywila, nambari ya kitambulisho au hati zako, jumbe au taarifa za M-Pesa, maelezo ya benki, **picha zozote za skrini**, wala salio lako halisi. Tafadhali usizitume, hata kama ungependa kutusaidia." |
| 3 Test with made-up numbers | "Later you'll try the app on **our** test phone using **made-up numbers**. We are testing the app, **not you**. If something is confusing, that's the app's fault, and exactly what we need to learn." | "Baadaye utajaribu programu kwenye simu **yetu** ya majaribio ukitumia **nambari za kubuni**. Tunajaribu programu, **si wewe**. Kama kitu kinachanganya, ni kosa la programu, na ndicho tunachohitaji kujifunza." |
| 4 What we collect | "We will write **notes** about what you tell us and how the test goes, using a **code instead of your name**. Your phone number is used only to arrange the meeting and send the thank-you." | "Tutaandika **maelezo** kuhusu unachotueleza na jinsi majaribio yanavyokwenda, tukitumia **namba ya siri badala ya jina lako**. Nambari yako ya simu inatumika tu kupanga mkutano na kukutumia shukrani." |
| 5 Who sees it, storage, deletion | "Only the owner of this project and the interviewer can see these notes. They are kept in a private place, never published with your name. Phone numbers are deleted within 30 days after the study ends, and coded notes within 6 months. You can ask us to delete your information at any time before we write our final summary." | "Ni mmiliki wa mradi huu na mhojaji pekee wanaoweza kuona maelezo haya. Yanahifadhiwa mahali pa faragha, na hayachapishwi kamwe pamoja na jina lako. Nambari za simu zinafutwa ndani ya siku 30 baada ya utafiti kuisha, na maelezo yenye namba ya siri ndani ya miezi 6. Unaweza kutuomba tufute taarifa zako wakati wowote kabla hatujaandika muhtasari wetu wa mwisho." |
| 6 Audio (optional) | "**May we take notes?** And, only if you agree, may we **record audio**? We'd delete it within 7 days, after writing our notes. Saying no is completely fine." | "**Tunaweza kuandika maelezo?** Na, ukikubali tu, tunaweza **kurekodi sauti**? Tutaifuta ndani ya siku 7, baada ya kuandika maelezo. Kukataa ni sawa kabisa." |
| 7 Voluntary | "Taking part is your choice. You can **skip any question** and **stop at any time**, without giving a reason." | "Kushiriki ni hiari yako. Unaweza **kuruka swali lolote** na **kusimama wakati wowote**, bila kueleza sababu." |
| 8 Equal thank-you | "Everyone receives the **same thank-you** of KSh ____, **whatever you say, and even if you stop early.**" | "Kila mtu anapokea **shukrani ileile** ya KSh ____, **bila kujali utakachosema, hata ukisimama mapema.**" |
| 9 Questions | "Do you have any questions? Are you happy to continue?" | "Una swali lolote? Uko tayari kuendelea?" |

## 7-day trial consent (voluntary; offered only at the end of the interview)

| # | English | Kiswahili |
|---|---|---|
| T1 Voluntary | "This is **optional**. Saying no doesn't change anything, and you still receive the thank-you you've been promised." | "Hili ni **la hiari**. Kukataa hakubadilishi chochote, na bado utapokea shukrani uliyoahidiwa." |
| T2 Your records stay on your phone | "You'd use the app on **your own phone**. Anything you type into the app **stays on your phone; we can't see it.** You can delete it any time: Settings → Delete all data." | "Utatumia programu kwenye simu **yako mwenyewe**. Chochote utakachoandika kwenye programu **kinabaki kwenye simu yako; sisi hatuwezi kukiona.** Unaweza kukifuta wakati wowote: Settings → Delete all data." |
| T3 WhatsApp replies are research information | "Each evening we'll send **one short WhatsApp question** from a study number. **Your replies are information we collect for the research.** We copy them into our notes under your code, delete the chat from our study phone, and protect them as explained earlier." | "Kila jioni tutatuma **swali moja fupi la WhatsApp** kutoka nambari ya utafiti. **Majibu yako ni taarifa tunazokusanya kwa ajili ya utafiti.** Tunayanakili kwenye maelezo yetu chini ya namba yako ya siri, tunafuta mazungumzo kwenye simu yetu ya utafiti, na tunayalinda kama tulivyoeleza awali." |
| T4 No screenshots or private details | "Please **don't send screenshots**, M-Pesa messages, balances or amounts. Just the short answers we ask for." | "Tafadhali **usitume picha za skrini**, jumbe za M-Pesa, salio au viwango vya pesa. Majibu mafupi tunayouliza tu." |
| T5 Leave any time | "You can **stop the trial at any time**, without a reason and **without any penalty**. Just tell us, or stop replying." | "Unaweza **kuacha majaribio wakati wowote**, bila sababu na **bila adhabu yoyote**. Tuambie tu, au acha kujibu." |
| T6 Equal thank-you | "Everyone who **starts** the trial receives the **same trial thank-you** of KSh ____, **even if you leave early or record very little.**" | "Kila anayeanza majaribio anapokea **shukrani ileile ya majaribio** ya KSh ____, **hata ukiacha mapema au ukiandika kidogo sana.**" |
| T7 Agreement | "Would you like to join the 7-day trial?" | "Ungependa kujiunga na majaribio ya siku 7?" |
